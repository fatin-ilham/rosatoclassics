## Typography

### Primary Font (Headings/Quotes)
**Playfair Display**  
*Serif, elegant, Italian heritage feel*

- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), Italic (400)
- Usage: H1–H4, quotes, featured text
- Style: Timeless, premium, trustworthy

### Secondary Font (Body/UI)
**Inter**  
*Sans-serif, clean, modern*

- Weights: Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500)
- Usage: Body copy, navigation, buttons, labels
- Style: Readable, neutral, professional

### Font Pairing Rationale
- **Playfair Display** = Heritage, legitimacy, Italian-American sophistication
- **Inter** = Modern, clean, no-nonsense business approach
- Together = "Old school values, new school execution"

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- Confident but not arrogant
- Warm but professional
- Minimal words, maximum impact
- Family business energy

### Do's
✓ Short sentences  
✓ Direct language  
✓ Focus on relationships and trust  
✓ Emphasize quality and fairness  
✓ Use "we" and "our community"

### Don'ts
✗ No hype or exaggeration  
✗ No slang or street talk  
✗ No mention of scarcity tactics  
✗ No corporate speak  
✗ No flashy claims

### Example Copy
> "We've been serving our community for years. Fair prices, honest deals, treating people right. That's the Rosato way."

---

## Visual Style

### Photography
- Dark, moody lighting
- Clean backgrounds (concrete, black, grey)
- Product-focused, minimal props
- No models faces needed — focus on shoes
- Slight film grain for texture (optional)

### Layout Principles
- Generous white (negative) space
- Strong grid structure
- Centered compositions for hero sections
- Asymmetric grids for product displays
- Borders and dividers for structure

### Iconography
- Minimal line icons only
- 1–2px stroke weight
- No filled icons
- Monochrome only

## Brand Applications

### Website
- Dark theme throughout

- Black boxes, white text
